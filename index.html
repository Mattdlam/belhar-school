About Our Institution

Welcome to the official portal of Belhar Islamic Primary School. Located in the Western Cape, South Africa, our institution provides comprehensive primary education integrated with foundational Islamic values. We serve learners from Grade R through Grade 7, fostering academic excellence, spiritual growth, and community responsibility.

Our Vision

To produce balanced, exemplary citizens who excel academically and are firmly grounded in their faith, positively contributing to the broader South African society.

Curriculum

We strictly follow the national CAPS curriculum mandated by the Department of Basic Education, enriched with specialized Islamic Studies and Arabic language tracks.
